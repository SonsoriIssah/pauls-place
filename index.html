About Paul's Place

A Premier Address
in Dzorwulu, Accra

Paul's Place Complex is a distinguished residential and commercial development in the prestigious Dzorwulu neighbourhood. Two elegant blocks rise five floors each — offering 10 meticulously designed apartments alongside ground-floor commercial spaces in a secure, gated environment.

Every unit is thoughtfully finished with modern open-plan kitchens, premium tiled floors, walk-in closets, private balconies, and full air conditioning — setting a new standard for urban living in Accra.
