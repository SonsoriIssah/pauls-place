About Paul's Place

A Premier Address in Dzorwulu, Accra

Paul's Place Complex is a distinguished residential and commercial development nestled in the prestigious Dzorwulu neighbourhood of Accra. Comprising two elegant blocks rising five floors, the complex offers 10 meticulously designed apartments alongside ground-floor commercial spaces — all within a secure, gated environment.

Every unit is thoughtfully finished with modern open-plan kitchens, premium tiled floors, walk-in closets, private balconies, and complete air conditioning — setting a new standard for urban living in Accra.
