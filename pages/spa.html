Resense Spa

A Journey Into Wellness

The Resense Spa at Kempinski Hotel Gold Coast City is a sanctuary of transformation. Across three dedicated floors, our expert therapists guide you through a deeply personalised journey — drawing on ancient healing traditions and modern wellness science to restore balance and vitality.

Whether you seek the profound relaxation of our signature Resense massage, the revitalising power of our hydrotherapy circuits, or the focused intensity of a personal training session — your wellness journey begins here.
